*March 16th - 18th 2026*

All vehicles and trailers must be removed from W. 10th St. (between Johnson Dr. and S. Tyler) and S. Harrison Ave. (between W. 4th and W. 6th). This notice is effective from Monday, March 16, through Wednesday, March 18. Thank you for your cooperation.

We will be performing a controlled brush burn at our wastewater plant at 2829 S. Main Street on Friday, February 27th, early in the morning. Please be advised that you may see smoke in the area. This is a planned activity and there is no cause for alarm.

The new cemetery gates have been completed.
The gates will be closed nightly at dark and reopened each morning at dawn.
We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

The Splash Pad is now closed for the season.
But mark your calendars! We will reopen for the McGregor ISD Spring Break!

Join us on **Saturday, October 18th** for a day filled with vintage aircraft, community spirit, and a celebration of the golden age of flight.
McGregor Executive Airport
