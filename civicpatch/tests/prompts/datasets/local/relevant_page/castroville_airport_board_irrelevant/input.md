Airport Advisory Board
======================

The Airport Advisory Board was originally established after July 1949 Â and was reestablished March 2009 to review and assist city staff and city council in researching and analyzing issues impacting the airport. The board will help promote the airport, assist in development of airport improvement plans, and make recommendations as necessary. The board consists of five members, two alternate positions, and the members may reside outside the city limits.
The Airport Advisory Board has no restrictions on residency. Â Terms are for two years ending June 30th of each year.
The Airport Advisory Board meets the 1st Monday of the Month at 5 p.m. unless otherwise noted on the calendar.
