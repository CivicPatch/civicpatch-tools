# Maine: towns and plantations

Step 2 of the `state-offices` skill. Layers: [2026-11-20-research-me-layers.md](2026-11-20-research-me-layers.md).
Statute pages: Revisor's text, extracted 10/05/2026. MMA: Maine Municipal Association,
[Local Government in Maine](https://www.memun.org/Documents?Command=Core_Download&EntryId=577), Aug. 1, 2016,
chapter 4, "Forms of Local Government".

## Forms and how many towns use each

MMA names five forms covering every Maine municipality; four apply to towns.

| Form (MMA) | Legislative body | Executive | Towns (MMA 2016) | Law |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Town Meeting - Selectmen | town meeting | elected select board | 170 | 30-A § 2001(9)(A), § 2526(4) |
| Town Meeting - Selectmen - Manager | town meeting | select board, appointed manager or administrator | "140-plus" with managers; 216 with a manager, administrator or administrative assistant | § 2631 to § 2636, or charter |
| Town Meeting - Council - Manager | town meeting (budget) and council (ordinances) | council, appointed manager | 18 | charter |
| Council - Manager | town council | council, appointed manager | 24 | charter |

MMA on the council form: "Unlike town meeting government, which existed at statehood, the council form must be
adopted through a local charter." No statute provides a charterless council or a representative town meeting (chapters
121 and 123 indexes, [§ 2101](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2101.html)).

**How a town's form is told:** not by its name; every one is "X town". A council town has a charter; a charterless
town is town meeting and select board, with or without a manager. Only the town's own records or charter say which.

## Organizations and roles

### Select board: legislative-executive, listed

- Elected, 3, 5 or 7; the town fixes the number and term, else 3 for one year
  ([§ 2526(4)(A)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2526.html)).
- They are the town's "municipal officers" ([§ 2001(10)(A)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2001.html))
  and appoint all officials required by law "Except where specifically provided by law, charter or ordinance"
  ([§ 2601(1)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2601.html)). MMA: they "perform the executive
  function" and hold some ordinance powers (vehicles, public ways, general assistance).
- Under the manager plan the select board "as a body shall exercise all administrative and executive powers of the
  town" ([§ 2635](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2635.html)).
- Chair: a presiding office the members choose, so a member role, not a post.

### Town council: legislative-executive, listed (charter towns)

Set by each charter: MMA, "most communities elect five, seven or nine", at large, by ward, or mixed. A council
chair or a "mayor" chosen by the council is a member role. Pittsfield, a town, "does choose a mayor" (MMA).

### Town manager: executive, listed (appointed)

- Manager plan: "The select board shall choose the town manager"
  ([§ 2632](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2632.html)); "the chief executive and
  administrative official of the town" ([§ 2636(1)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2636.html)).
- Charter towns set the manager in their charter (MMA).
- A town administrator or administrative assistant has no statute: "There is no state law providing for an
  administrative assistant" (MMA). Not listed; the select board stays the executive.

### Town meeting: the legislative body, no posts

Every voter is a member ([§ 2001(9)(A)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2001.html)). The
moderator is elected by written ballot at each meeting and "shall serve until the meeting is adjourned"
([§ 2524(2)(A), (3)(C)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2524.html)): a presiding office the
members choose for one meeting, so not a post. Not listed.

### Offices that carry out tasks: not listed (rule 5)

| Office | Selection | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| Town clerk | elected by ballot, one year unless set ([§ 2526(9)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2526.html), [§ 2601(2)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2601.html)); may be appointed under the manager plan ([§ 2631(2)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2631.html)) | records votes, prepares ballots, records oaths ([§ 2524(4), (5)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2524.html)) |
| Treasurer, tax collector | elected or appointed; may be one person ([§ 2526(8)(A)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2526.html)) | keep and collect money under state law; a town may discontinue them and give the duties to the manager (MMA) |
| Assessors | elected board of 3, 5 or 7, or one appointed assessor ([§ 2526(5)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2526.html)) | value property; select board often serves (MMA) |
| Overseers of the poor | elected, else the select board serves ([§ 2526(4)(B)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2526.html)) | administer general assistance |
| Road commissioners | chosen by the town, else one; select board may act ([§ 2526(7)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2526.html)) | maintain roads |
| Constables | appointed ([§ 2673](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2673.html)) | serve warrants, keep order at meetings |
| School committee | elected ([§ 2631(2)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec2631.html)) | special-purpose board (education) |

Vermont's config lists its town clerk and treasurer; Maine's leaves them out (decided 2026-11-20): neither votes or
decides policy, the clerk only runs and records the meeting.

## Plantations

28 in open-data. They run under town law "as far as applicable"
([§ 7006](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec7006.html)) but have no home rule power, so no
charter (MMA). The annual meeting is the legislative body; 3 assessors are elected by ballot
([§ 7001(4)](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec7001.html)) and "are considered the members of
the select board of the plantation" ([§ 7007](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec7007.html)).
One form; told by the suffix "plantation".

## Net result

- **Forms:** `open_town_meeting` (town meeting, select board, manager where adopted) and `council_manager`
  (charter towns), both on the suffix `town`, so a run's pick or a person saves each town's form. Plantations:
  one form on the suffix `plantation`.
- **Organizations and roles:**
  - Select Board: Select Board Member (elected; § 2526(4)), Chair and Vice Chair as member roles.
  - Town Council (council towns): Council Member (elected; charter), Chair or Mayor as member roles.
  - Town Officers: Town Manager (appointed; § 2632, or charter).
  - Plantation Board of Assessors: Assessor (elected; § 7001(4), § 7007), Chair as a member role.
- **Left out:** moderator (per meeting, § 2524), clerk, treasurer, tax collector, assessors (in towns), overseers,
  road commissioners, constables (task offices), school committee (special-purpose), administrative assistant (no
  statute).
