# Maine: counties

Statute pages: Revisor's text, extracted 10/05/2026. MMA: Maine Municipal Association,

"County Government".

## One form: a board of commissioners

- [§ 61](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec61.html): "There shall be a board of commissioners
  for each county consisting of a chairman and 2 other persons"; members "shall be elected by the voters of that
  district", each representing one district.
- [§ 61-A](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec61-A.html): York, "a chair and 4 other citizens".
- [§ 67](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec67.html) draws 7 districts in Androscoggin, 5 in
  Cumberland, Franklin (from Nov. 5, 2024), Somerset and York, 3 elsewhere. MMA: "The citizens in each county
  typically elect three commissioners (four counties have larger boards of commissioners)". So boards follow the
  district count; no section found states the size for the four besides York.
- Chair: "The commissioners shall select their chairman annually" (§ 61(3)): a member role.
- The commissioners make the county estimates and assess the tax
  ([§ 701](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec701.html)); some counties have budget committees
  by their own sections (§§ 723, 751, 823, 831, 853), special-purpose, not listed.

Every county uses this form; told by the suffix `county`.

## County administrator or manager: executive, listed (appointed)

[§ 82](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec82.html): the commissioners "may appropriate funds
for the hiring of a county administrator", who "is the chief administrative official of the county"; Cumberland
(§ 82(5)) and York (§ 82(6)) "shall hire a full-time county manager".

## Other elected county officers

MMA: "counties have a treasurer, sheriff, judge of probate, register of probate and a register of deeds, all of
them elected."

| Office | Law | Listed? |
|---|---|---|
| Treasurer | elected, 4 years ([§ 151](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec151.html)); commissioners may make it appointed with voter approval ([§ 156](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec156.html)) | no: keeps money (rule 5) |
| Sheriff | "elected or appointed ... according to the Constitution of Maine" ([§ 371-B](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec371-B.html)) | no: runs the jail and patrols on the commissioners' budget (§ 701), not the county government (decided 2026-11-20; Oregon lists its sheriff) |
| Register of deeds | elected, 4 years ([33 M.R.S. § 601](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/33/title33sec601.html)) | no: records deeds (rule 5) |
| Judge of probate | elected ([4 M.R.S. § 301](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/4/title4sec301.html)) | no: a court |
| Register of probate | elected (MMA) | no: court records (rule 5) |
| District attorney | elected by prosecutorial district ([§ 251](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec251.html)) | no: a state office |

## Charters

Chapter 11 lets a county's voters adopt a charter that "may determine the officers of the county"
([§ 1301](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/30-A/title30-Asec1301.html)). **Somerset County** has one: adopted by
its voters in November 2010, it widened the board from 3 to 5
([Bangor Daily News, 2010-10-27](https://www.bangordailynews.com/2010/10/27/news/somerset-county-voters-to-weigh-larger-board-of-commissioners/);
text: [Charter-2024.pdf](https://somersetcountyme.gov/wp-content/uploads/Charter-2024.pdf)). Pipeline 1 found it on
2026-11-20; this research had missed it. Others may exist: the statewide law read is the check.

## Net result

- **Form:** `commission` on the suffix `county`.
- **Organizations and roles:** Board of County Commissioners: Commissioner (elected by district; § 61, § 67), Chair
  as a member role (§ 61(3)). County Officers: County Administrator or Manager (appointed; § 82).
- **Left out:** treasurer, sheriff, registers of deeds and probate (task offices), judge of probate (court), district attorney
  (state office), budget committees (special-purpose).
