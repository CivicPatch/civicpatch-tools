### Members

The governing body, known as the City Council, consists of a Mayor and five Council Members, who are elected by places.

Tom
Leverentz
Mayor
Read Bio
×

I graduated from high school in Pennsylvania and later attended college in Nebraska. I then served in the Army as a Warhead and Guidance Specialist on the Nike Hercules Missile System. After my honorable discharge from the Army, I worked for Asplundh Tree Expert Company. I was promoted to Vice President and transferred to Texas. My family and I lived in Duncanville until 1996 when we then moved to Ovilla. I worked as a volunteer fireman for the City of Ovilla leaving only to serve on the city council. I then served as Mayor Pro Tem and was elected mayor and served my full term. In 2012, I retired from Asplundh after 47 years and moved to Oak Leaf. It was then I accepted the position of fire chief for the City of Ferris. A few years later, I once again retired and now spend time with my grandchildren and raise longhorn cows. In May of 2019, I was elected as an Oak Leaf council member, and in May of 2020, I became mayor and have served in that capacity since then.

[![email](https://www.oakleaftexas.org/wp-content/plugins/team-members/inc/img/links/email.png)](mailto:tleverentz@oakleaftexas.org "email")
Mitchell
Burnett
Place 1
Read Bio
×

Mitchell Burnett has been a proud resident of Oak Leaf, Texas, for over 16 years. He and his wife Bobbi have two adult children that were raised in this exceptional small town. His dedication to this rural community is evident in his commitment to preserving its unique character and aligning city administration with the needs and objectives of its citizens. Deeply invested in maintaining Oak Leaf’s rural charm, Mitchell believes that thoughtful development and community engagement are essential to enhancing the quality of life for all residents. Mitchell’s career is complemented by nine years of distinguished service in the United States Air Force, culminating in a prestigious assignment with the White House Communication Agency. In this role, he had the honor of supporting the President, Vice President, and First Lady, further instilling in him a profound sense of duty to service and community. Currently employed with the Army & Air Force Exchange Service (Exchange), Mitchell brings over three decades of extensive experience in leadership, government acquisition and executive management to his city council role. The Exchange, established in 1895, is a vital non-appropriated fund entity of the Department of Defense, ranking as the 52nd-largest retail organization in the U.S. With annual revenues of $8.5 billion and approximately 26,000 associates, including military personnel, the Exchange plays a significant role in supporting military families and personnel stationed across the globe. As Vice President of Corporate Procurement at the Exchange, Mitchell oversees a contracting organization that manages a wide-ranging portfolio of over 2,700 contracts valued at more than $13 billion. He is responsible for the acquisition of a broad array of concessionaire services and the procurement of all expense category requirements including information technology, logistics, professional services, equipment & fixtures and construction/design/facility maintenance services - ensuring the continued operation and maintenance and improvement of over 5,500 facilities, retail stores, restaurants, and recreational services on military installations. As a member of the City Council, Mitchell leverages his extensive background in management, business operations and community service to further Oak Leaf’s community objectives by advocating for responsible growth, reasonable community oversight and common sense solutions while preserving individual liberty. His mission is to ensure that Oak Leaf remains a welcoming and vibrant rural community for future generations, fostering an environment where residents can thrive while cherishing the distinct qualities that have long defined our city. Mitchell earned a Master of Business Administration from Texas A&M University-Commerce and a bachelor’s degree in management from National Louis University. His Air Force decorations include the Air Medal, Aerial Achievement Medal with one oak leaf cluster, Joint Service Commendation Medal, Air Force Commendation Medal with one oak leaf cluster. He has served on City Council since 2022.

Michelle
Hillery
Place 4
Read Bio
×

Michelle Hillery was sworn in as the Place 4 Council member to fill the vacancy of Dustin Jordan and was voted into the Council on March 8. As the appointed Chairperson of the first Oak Leaf Planning and Zoning Commission in 2021 to bring citizen transparency to planned development requests, she provided leadership and experience in engineering and impact assessment analysis in the plat review and recommendation process. She moved to Oak Leaf in September 1995 and has two grown children and one Granddaughter. She earned a Bachelor of Political Science and Master of Public Administration degree from Texas A&M University in College Station. She comes from a law enforcement and veteran family and is very proud to serve her constituents and uphold the Constitution. She has an extensive Technology and Financial Services career as an Architect, Consultant and Senior Executive. She retired from IBM in 2007 and currently works for a Financial Services Company. Hillery believes in government transparency and accountability. She enjoys her farm and raising her livestock and she cherishes the country environment that Oak Leaf offers for future generations. She would like to continue to facilitate communication with the citizens and encourage citizen participation in the City Council and Planning and Zoning Commission meetings. She looks forward to meeting with citizens and understand their vision for the City so that she can represent them more effectively. Michelle has served on City Council since 2023.
