# General law

- **§ 1304. First meeting; officers**
(b) At such meeting, or at an adjournment thereof, the corporation shall elect a presiding officer, a clerk, five trustees, a treasurer, and a collector of taxes and may elect a tree warden and chief engineer who shall hold their offices until the first annual meeting and until others are elected and qualified. Any qualified and eligible voter of the town in which such village is situated may be elected as such tax collector, chief engineer, clerk, treasurer, or presiding officer.

# Bylaws

- **Chapter 2, Article 1. Officers**
Section 1. The officers of Johnson Village shall be as follows: A board of three Trustees; a board of three Auditors; a President; a Clerk; a Treasurer; a "Collector and Constable" and a "Water and Light Commissioner".
- **Chapter 2, Article 2. Election of Officers**
Section 1. At each annual meeting the Village shall elect, by ballot if called for by three legal voters, the following officers. One Trustee; one Auditor, a President, a Clerk, and a Treasurer.
- **Chapter 2, Article 3. Terms of Officers**
Section 1. The terms of the Village President, Clerk, Treasurer, "Collector and Constable" and the "Water and Light Commissioner" shall be for one year, from the date of their election. The terms of the Village Trustees and the Auditors shall be for three years from the date of their election.
- **Chapter 2, Article 4. Duties of Officers**
Section 1. Trustees. B. The Trustees shall immediately after the annual meeting appoint from among the Board, a Chairman, and shall file such appointment with the Village Clerk for record. Such chairman to preside at all annual or special village meetings in the absence of the President.
Section 2. President. A. The President shall preside at all Village Meetings held during his term of office. He shall call all meetings to order and make public declaration of all votes cast.
