**Republic, Washington is a city with an early 1900s look and a heritage based in the mining and logging industries.**

Founded by gold prospectors, and originally named Eureka Gulch, Republic had a population of 1,073 in 2010. Noted for its fossils, natural beauty and recreational possibilities, it is nestled in a valley between Wauconda and Sherman Passes…
