Boards & Commissions City Commission The City Commission meets on the fourth Tuesday of each month at 5:30 p.m. in the conference/court room at City Hall.   > [View Calendar of Upcoming Events](https://www.cityofadamsville.com/upcoming_events/index.html) The City Commission consisting of the Mayor and four Commissioners govern the City of Adamsville. At large elections are held on the first Tuesday in November in each even numbered year for the positions whose terms of office is expiring. The Mayor and Commissioners serve four year staggered terms.
