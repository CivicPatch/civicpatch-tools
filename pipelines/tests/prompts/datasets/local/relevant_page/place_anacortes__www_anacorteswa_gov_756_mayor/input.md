Mayor
=====

Mayor
**Ryan Walters**
took office on January 1, 2026.
[Learn more about Mayor Walters](https://www.anacorteswa.gov/760)
or about
[earlier mayors of Anacortes](https://www.anacorteswa.gov/779/Past-Mayors-PDF)
.

In a mayor-council non-charter code city like Anacortes, the mayor is the chief executive and oversees all operations, utilities, employees, and the city's annual budget of more than $100 million. The mayor also:

* presides over council meetings;
* has the authority to break some City Council tie votes;
* recommends City Council legislation;
* can veto City Council legislation (subject to override);
* proposes the City's preliminary budget;
* appoints department heads and most
  [boards and committees](https://www.anacorteswa.gov/160)
  .
