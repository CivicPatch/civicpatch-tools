### From the Mayor

Welcome to Bridgeport!
I’m Sergio Orozco, and it’s my honor to serve as the Mayor of our vibrant and diverse city. On behalf of the entire Bridgeport community, I extend a warm welcome to you.
Bridgeport is a city rich in history, culture, and opportunity. Whether you’re a lifelong resident, a newcomer, or just visiting, we are excited to share the unique charm and spirit that define our city.
Feel free to explore our website to learn more about what makes Bridgeport special.
Thank you for visiting. Together, let’s continue to make Bridgeport a place we are all proud to call home.

- Sergio Orozco,
Mayor
