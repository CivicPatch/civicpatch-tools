Government
==========

The City Of Manchester is committed to providing exceptional services to enhance the quality of life for all citizens through the efficient use of resources.

Manchester is a vibrant, progressive community that still retains traditional southern charm and hospitality. Our mission as a City government is to continually enhance this uniqueness by providing quality municipal services to our citizens. The City of Manchester recognizes that our community enjoys a special quality of life and that People are its greatest resource. Manchester provides ethical, innovative, and responsible service so that everyone can enjoy the benefits Manchester has to offer. City employees are involved in an active partnership with Manchester's citizens to foster educational, cultural and economic opportunities in a safe, vibrant and healthy community. Manchester's commitment to excellence, innovation and service, combined with sound fiscal management, will ensure its prominence in the 21st century. Our mission will be accomplished through efficient use of resources.
