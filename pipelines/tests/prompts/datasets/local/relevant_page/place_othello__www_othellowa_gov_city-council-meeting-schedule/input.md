City Council Meeting Schedule

City Council Meetings
are the 2
and 4
Monday of every month - 6:00 PM - City Hall Council Chambers (500 E. Main Street).

See the
[Calendar](https://www.othellowa.gov/calendar)
to check the status of specific meetings and to find recordings and approved minutes.

Agendas are available on
[Diligent](https://othellowa.community.highbond.com/Portal/)
