## News & Announcements

### Notice of Early Voting — May 2025 Election

Early voting for the May 3, 2025 Uniform Election will be held April 22–29, 2025 at Olton City Hall, 801 8th Street.

---

### Application for Landfill Permit

Notice is hereby given that the City of Olton has submitted an application to the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) for a municipal solid waste landfill permit. Comments may be submitted within 30 days.

---

### Roofing Permit Requirements

All roofing work within the city limits requires a permit. Permits may be obtained at City Hall during regular business hours, Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm.

---

### Annual Water Quality Report

The City of Olton is pleased to present its Annual Water Quality Report. This report provides information on your local drinking water quality. For more information, contact Public Works at (806) 285-2613.

---

### Approved FY2025 Budget

The City of Olton City Council adopted the FY2025 Annual Budget at the September 10, 2024 meeting. A copy of the budget is available for public inspection at City Hall.

---

### Notice of Intention to Issue Certificates of Obligation

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the City Council of the City of Olton, Texas intends to authorize the issuance of certificates of obligation in an amount not to exceed $1,200,000 for the purpose of financing improvements to the city's water and wastewater system.

### Tax Rate Increase Notice — FY2025

NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING ON TAX INCREASE

A tax rate of $0.589576 per $100 valuation has been proposed by the governing body of the City of Olton.

**PROPOSED TAX RATE:** $0.589576 per $100
**NO-NEW-REVENUE TAX RATE:** $0.527819 per $100
**VOTER-APPROVAL TAX RATE:** $0.578433 per $100

The proposed tax rate is greater than the voter-approval tax rate.

The following members of the City Council voted FOR the proposal to consider adopting a tax rate above the voter-approval rate:

FOR the proposal: Steven Rodriquez, Alderman; Blake Neinast, Alderman; Alan Williams, Alderman

AGAINST the proposal: None

PRESENT and not voting: Mark McFadden, Mayor

ABSENT: None
