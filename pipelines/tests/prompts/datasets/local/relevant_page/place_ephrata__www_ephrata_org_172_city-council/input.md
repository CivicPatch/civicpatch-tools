### Speaking at a City Council Meeting

If you would like to speak at a city council meeting, please fill out this
[Agenda Request Form (PDF)](https://www.ephrata.org/DocumentCenter/View/559)
and drop it off, mail it, or email it to us. You may want to read the official
[City Council Procedures (PDF)](https://www.ephrata.org/DocumentCenter/View/561)
to have a better understanding of what to expect. If you have a complaint that you would like to bring to the city council, please fill out a
[Citizen Comment Form (PDF)](https://www.ephrata.org/DocumentCenter/View/560)
and
[email the city clerk](mailto:%20rmiller@ephrata.org)
.

### Archival City Council Minutes & Ordinances

The Washington State Archives has collections of the minutes of the Ephrata City Council meetings from 1914 through 2009 and ordinances from 1901 through 2010. The Collection is titled
[Ephrata City Council, Minutes, 1914 to 2009](https://digitalarchives.wa.gov/Collections/TitleInfo/2075)
and
[Ephrata City Council, Ordinances, 1901 to 2010](https://digitalarchives.wa.gov/Collections/TitleInfo/2076)
.
