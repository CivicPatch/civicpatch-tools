City Councilmembers
===================

The City Council includes:

* three members elected from each of the city's three wards; and
* four at-large members elected from anywhere in the city.

If you would like to contact all of the city councilmembers and mayor simultaneously you can
[email the entire City Council](mailto:citycouncil@anacorteswa.gov)
