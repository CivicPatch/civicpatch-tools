City Council
============

* First and third Monday of each month
* City Council Chambers
  City Hall
  501 N. Anderson St.
  Ellensburg, WA 92986
* Attend virtually via Zoom or watch on the
  [ECTV YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC1ylXV9JtOa_RFco1w_jjSg)
  . The Zoom link for the meeting will be found in the published agenda packet.

The most recent agendas and minutes may be found below. To locate an older agenda or minutes, email
[City Clerk](mailto:leaderb@ci.ellensburg.wa.us)
or call (509) 925-8614.

If you would like to have the City Council agenda emailed to you ahead of the meeting, please fill out this
[form](https://www.ellensburgwa.gov/FormCenter/Council-Agenda-Signup-Form-19/City-Council-Agenda-Signup-Form-121)
, and you will be added to the distribution list.

[these instructions](https://ci.ellensburg.wa.us/DocumentCenter/View/18337/Agenda-System-How-To)
.

The City of Ellensburg utilizes the Council-
[Manager](https://ci.ellensburg.wa.us/index.aspx?NID=820 "Manager")
form of government with a City Manager hired by the City Council. The City Council elects a Mayor and Mayor Pro Tem from the Council to serve two-year terms.

Council Commitment
------------------

At the June 15, 2026 City Council meeting, the City Council approved
[Resolution 2026-13](https://www.ellensburgwa.gov/ArchiveCenter/ViewFile/Item/3638)
, which is a commitment to community values, listening and building connections, civil discourse, access and inclusion, and moving forward together.

As elected representatives, we invite all residents and visitors to join us in a shared commitment to making Ellensburg an inviting, equitable, safe, and united community for everyone.

The City Council affirms its commitment to continuing the work of listening, engaging, and building relationships across our community in a spirit of understanding, respect, and unity.

The City Council is committed to fostering a community where respectful and thoughtful political discourse, as well as peaceful dissent, are valued. At the same time, the Council firmly rejects violence, intimidation, and threats against any individual or group.

The City Council further resolves to work in partnership with its advisory boards and commissions, community organizations, and community members to incorporate principles of access, equity, engagement, and inclusion (full participation) into City programs, services, facilities, policies, and practices for all members of our community.

The City Council is committed to working collaboratively with all members of the community to move forward in a manner that ensures every person is heard, respected, and valued.
