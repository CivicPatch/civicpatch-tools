The Airport Advisory Commission plays a critical role in ensuring the success and growth of the San Saba Municipal Airport. As an essential part of the city's infrastructure, the airport supports regional connectivity, economic development, and access to vital services for the community and surrounding areas.

The commission provides valuable recommendations to the City Council on airport operations, maintenance, and improvements. Members work closely with city staff, aviation experts, and stakeholders to ensure the airport meets safety standards, accommodates the needs of pilots and passengers, and remains a valuable resource for San Saba.

From planning upgrades to maintaining a welcoming and functional facility, the Airport Advisory Commission is dedicated to fostering a vibrant and efficient airport that serves as a gateway to the City of San Saba. Residents and businesses alike benefit from the commission's commitment to supporting local aviation and encouraging opportunities for growth.
