Mayor & Council
===============

The City of Ames Mayor and City Council work together for the citizens of our community. As leaders, they are committed to protecting the City of Ames' assets through the encouragement of residents and businesses to become integral participants in our community.

Public City Council meetings will be held on the 2nd Monday of each month. If an emergency occurs and the meeting cannot take place, then the rescheduled meeting time and date will be posted on the website. Please check the website frequently for such updates.
