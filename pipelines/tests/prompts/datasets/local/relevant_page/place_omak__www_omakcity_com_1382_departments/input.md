Departments
===========

![city_hall](local://66959ab9e486.png)
The City of Omak was incorporated in 1911.  We are a code city and operate under a mayor-council form of government.  The City of Omak employs an Administrator who reports to the Mayor.  The City Administrator oversees the department heads and manages day-to-day operations.  The city departments include: building, finance, fire, police, and public works.  We all work together to provide a great community for our citizens and visitors.
