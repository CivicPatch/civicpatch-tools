King County has 2 courts - District Court and Superior Court, whose judges are elected to 4 year terms.

### District Court

King County
[District Court](https://kingcounty.gov/en/court/district-court)
is a
[court of limited jurisdiction](https://www.courts.wa.gov/newsinfo/resources/?fa=newsinfo_jury.display&altMenu=Citi&folderID=jury_guide&fileID=limited)
, and has 25 elected judges who hear a range of civil and criminal cases at 10 locations throughout the County.

King County
[Superior Court](https://kingcounty.gov/en/court/superior-court)
is a
[general jurisdiction](https://kingcounty.gov/en/court/superior-court/about-superior-court/court-jurisdiction)
, and has more than 50 judges who hear a range of civil and criminal cases at 3 main locations.
