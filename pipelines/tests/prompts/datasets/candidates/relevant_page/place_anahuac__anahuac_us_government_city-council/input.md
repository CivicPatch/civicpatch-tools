City Council
============

City Council meetings are held the second Monday of each month at 6:00 p.m., unless the second Monday is a scheduled City holiday then the meeting will be held the second Tuesday of the month.

### Members

City Council positions are for two-year terms, City Election for positions on the City Council are held in May of each year.
