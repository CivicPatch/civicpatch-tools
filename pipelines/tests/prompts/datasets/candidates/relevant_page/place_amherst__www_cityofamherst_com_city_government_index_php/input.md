**Qualifications of Office of Mayor**
In Type C general law city, every candidate for the office of mayor must meet the following qualifications:

1. Have been a resident of Texas for at least 12 months, as of the deadline for filing for office;
2. Have resided in the city for at least 6 months preceding the election day;
3. Must be a registered voter in the territory elected from by the filing deadline;
4. Be 18 years of age or older upon the commencement of the term to be filled at the election;

(
*Election Code Section 141.001; Local Government Code Section 22.032 and 23.024)*

**Qualifications of Office of Councilmember**
In general law cities; the qualifications for the office of councilmember are:

1. Have been a resident of Texas for at least 12 months, as of the deadline for filing for office;
2. Have resided in the city for at least 6 months prior to filing deadline;
3. Must be a registered voter in the territory elected from by the filing deadline;
4. Be 18 years of age or older upon the commencement of the term to be filled at the election;

(Election Code Section 141.001; Local Government Code Section 22.032 and 23.024)
