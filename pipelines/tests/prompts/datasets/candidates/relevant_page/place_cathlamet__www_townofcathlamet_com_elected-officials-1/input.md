Elected Officials
=================

The Town of Cathlamet operates under the Mayor/Council form of government pursuant to
[Title 35.27 RCW](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=35.27)
, with an elected mayor and five nonpartisan council members each serving a 4-year term.
