Your Elected Officials
----------------------

Apple Valley has an elected Mayor and four Council districts. The Mayor leads all meetings. The Town Council also acts as the board for the Apple Valley Community Resource Foundation.

Elections are held every two years for the Mayor and Council seats that are ending. Council members serve for four years, and the Mayor serves for two years.

The Mayor leads Council meetings, and if the Mayor is absent, the Mayor Pro Tem takes over. Each December, the Council picks one member to be Mayor Pro Tem.

Guy Eisenbrey, Town Manager
---------------------------

Meet your Town Manager. Guy Eisenbrey is recognized for his steady leadership, strong emphasis on employees, and commitment to customer service. His work has spanned multiple departments and strategic priorities, helping position Apple Valley for continued growth and success.

“I am honored and grateful for the opportunity to serve as Town Manager,” said Eisenbrey. “Apple Valley is an incredible community, and it has been a privilege to be part of the team over the past several years. I look forward to continuing our work together to serve our residents and build on the momentum we’ve created.”
