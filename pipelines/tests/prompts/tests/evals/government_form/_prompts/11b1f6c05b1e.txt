
    Which form of government does this municipality use: Brookline town, Massachusetts

    Answer with exactly one of these values:
    - mayor_council: a council plus a separately elected mayor who does not sit on it
    - council_manager: a council with an appointed manager; any mayor sits on the council
    - commission: elected commissioners who together govern and each head a department
    - township_board: one elected board whose presiding officer sits and votes on it
    - open_town_meeting: a select board or board of selectmen, with an open town meeting
    - representative_town_meeting: a select board, with elected town meeting members

    The test between mayor_council and the one-board forms: does the chief executive sit and vote on the governing body? If they do, it is not mayor_council. Township committees, boards of supervisors or trustees, and New York towns and villages are township_board.
